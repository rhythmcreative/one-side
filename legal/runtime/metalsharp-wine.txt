MetalSharp Wine — https://github.com/metalsharp/MetalSharp-Wine

One-Side downloads MetalSharp Wine's x86_64 build (MetalSharp-Wine-x86_64.app) from that
project's GitHub releases the first time a Steam bottle is made, or when you pick the
D3DMetal engine for a bottle. It checks the download against the release's SHA256SUMS and
the app's Developer ID signature, and runs it unmodified. One-Side does not include or
change any of its code.

Apple D3DMetal (Game Porting Toolkit)
MetalSharp Wine carries Apple's D3DMetal from the Game Porting Toolkit, unmodified and
signed by Apple, under Apple's own license: Contents/share/licenses/d3dmetal/License.rtf
inside the app. One-Side does not distribute it. D3DMetal is Apple's; One-Side is not
affiliated with Apple or with MetalSharp.

Per-game fixes
The Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy fixes (the Shader Model 6.7 comparison and HDR10 off)
follow what MacGameFix (github.com/MathiasKowoll/MacGameFix) documented; One-Side's code
for them is its own.
